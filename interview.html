🎯 Interview Preparation

Prepare for HR and technical interviews.

HR Interview Questions

1. Tell me about yourself.

Introduce your education, skills, projects and career interests.

Start with your name and education. Explain your technical skills, academic projects, achievements and career goals. Keep your answer structured and around 1–2 minutes.

2. What are your strengths?

Mention strengths such as problem-solving, adaptability, teamwork and willingness to learn. Support them with a real example.

3. Why should we hire you?

Explain how your technical knowledge, communication skills and learning attitude can contribute to the company.

4. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Talk about becoming a skilled professional, taking responsibility and contributing to meaningful projects.

Technical Interview Questions

1. What is OOP?

Object-Oriented Programming is a programming approach based on objects and classes. Its four main principles are Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism and Abstraction.

2. What is DBMS?

DBMS stands for Database Management System. It is software used to store, manage, retrieve and manipulate data efficiently.

3. What is a Primary Key?

A Primary Key uniquely identifies each record in a database table. It cannot contain NULL values or duplicate values.

4. What is the difference between HTTP and HTTPS?

HTTP transfers data without transport-layer encryption. HTTPS uses TLS to encrypt communication between the client and server.

5. What is an Operating System?

An Operating System manages computer hardware and software resources and provides services for application programs.
